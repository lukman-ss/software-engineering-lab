# Contradictions

No material contradictions discovered.

### Minor Structural Discrepancies
- **Status Lifecycle:** Nygard explicitly mentions `Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Deprecated`, and `Superseded`. AWS Prescriptive Guidance adds an explicit `Rejected` state to capture proposed designs that the team chose not to pursue, avoiding re-evaluating rejected proposals.
- **Template Granularity:** Nygard limits sections strictly to Title, Context, Decision, Status, and Consequences. More modern templates (MADR / AWS / Richards & Ford) explicitly separate `Decision Drivers`, `Considered Options / Alternatives`, `Pros and Cons of the Options`, and `Review Triggers`. Both frameworks align on rationale, context, and consequences.
